Privacy policy

Effective November 10, 2026. Curvo does not collect, sell or share your personal data. It has no account, no analytics and no advertising.

What stays on your phone

Your recordings (GPS positions, speed, motion sensor data), cars, tracks, service history and settings are stored only on your device, inside the app. We do not receive them. Deleting the app deletes them.

Data leaves your phone only when you choose to: when you share a run, track or car file, export a report, or back up to a folder you pick in the Files app (for example iCloud Drive). Where that copy goes is up to you and the service you choose.

Permissions

  • Location: records your route, speed and times while you record, shows your distance to tracks and suggests a track when you are at its start. Used on the device only.
  • Motion & Fitness: g-forces and barometric elevation for your runs.
  • Bluetooth: connects to an external GPS receiver or an OBD2 adapter that you pair.

Online services Curvo talks to

To show maps and some details, the app sends requests to these third-party services. They see the request (including your IP address, and for some, the coordinates involved), as any website you open would. Curvo sends no identifiers of you or your device.

  • Map tiles: OpenFreeMap (map and labels) and AWS Open Data Terrarium tiles (terrain shading) for the area on screen.
  • Weather and elevation: Open-Meteo, with the coordinates of the track or your position, for the conditions of a run and the elevation of a track you draw.
  • Routing: the public OSRM server, with the points you tap when you draw a track, to follow the road.
  • Currency rates: open.er-api.com, for the service cost converter. No personal data is sent.

Their own privacy policies apply to these requests.

Children

Curvo is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
