Getting started

  • Add your car in the Garage, so every run keeps the car and its specs it was driven with.
  • Pick a track on Tracks, or draw your own: tap the start, the finish and a few points on the road.
  • Mount the phone, tap Start and drive. Each pass between the start and finish lines is timed on its own, both ways on a hill climb road and lap by lap on a circuit.

Location and sensors

  • Location, Precise Location: needed to time a run. Without Precise Location iOS reports a rough position about once a minute.
  • Always (optional): keeps a recording going while the phone is locked in its mount.
  • Motion & Fitness (optional): g-forces and barometric elevation.
  • Bluetooth (optional): an external GPS receiver (RaceBox) or an OBD2 adapter.

You can change any of these in Settings › Curvo.

Your data

Runs, cars and settings stay on your phone. Curvo has no account and no server. You can export a run as GPX, CSV or a Curvo session file, and back up to a folder you choose in Files. See the privacy policy.

Drive safe

A public road is not a race track. Time your runs at organised events, on closed roads and on circuits. Keep to the speed limit and the rules of the road everywhere else, wear your seatbelt, and keep your eyes on the road, not on the phone.
